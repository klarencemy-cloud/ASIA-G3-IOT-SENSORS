Galler.IO

Galler.IO is an IoT based monitoring system that collects and display real-time data. It features a dashboard where users can monitor the surrounding conditions, observe temperature and humidity, measure distance, detect water presence, as well as determine whether the environment is light or dark. Additionally, the IoT device is also equipped with a buzzer that activates when any of the five sensors reaches a predefined threshold or meet any condition, it alerts the users to conditions that may require attention or proper actions.
